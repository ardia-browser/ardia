Everything comes from the lab probe: it posts the Darwin notification com.ardia.browser.ios.lab.fullPage[.mode] and calls the
scene's real screenshotService.delegate the way iOS does. A mode disturbs the page in the next main-actor turn, while WebKit renders
the PDF: .navigate loads wiki/Beacon, .scroll scrolls 600 pt, .sheet presents a sheet, and .grow adds 3000 pt to the scroll view's
content height (same width) and scrolls to the new bottom, past the end of the PDF being made. .grow sets the native content size
directly to stand in for page content growing; it does not grow the DOM. The real screenshot editor was not shown here.
