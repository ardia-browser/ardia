## New

- **Ardia runs on Chromium 158.** The engine moves from 157 to 158.0.8127.36, with the security fixes that come with it.
  Nothing about your tabs, profiles or extensions changes.
- **Apple Passwords fills your logins, as a plugin.** Turn it on under Settings › Plugins › Official › Apple Passwords
  and a key appears in sign-in fields; click it, or use Edit › AutoFill › Passwords…, to open macOS's Passwords panel
  and fill with Touch ID. It works on Chrome-engine and WebKit pages, and can set up verification codes from a site's
  QR code. The panel lists all your logins, not only the site's, and passkeys saved in Passwords aren't offered yet.
  Thanks, [@kirolos-esmat](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2802).
- **Command Line 0.4.0 can drive everything in a profile.** `ardia call` and `ardia methods` reach the browser's own
  commands: folders, tiles, settings, extensions, sync, prompts and menus. Plugins' helpers now run in a tighter
  sandbox and can't launch other apps.
- **Drag a tab onto a row to split with it, or onto another profile to move it there.** Dragging in the sidebar shows a
  drop line and a see-through ghost, as in Arc, and profile dots can be dragged to reorder profiles. Thanks,
  [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2734).
- **Settings search finds a shortcut by its keys.** Type a combination such as ⌥⌘N or "cmd opt n" in Settings' search,
  or press it with the keyboard button in Settings › Shortcuts, and it shows the command that holds it.
- **Tabs across the top stay readable in a narrow window.** Titles keep a readable width; tabs that would shrink to a
  few letters move into the open tabs menu, and their group shows how many went there.
- **Peek opens and closes with a Genie effect**, and Open as Tab carries the page over without a flash. Thanks,
  [@TechSupportz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2168).
- **Local sites get Arc's striped developer bar.** Developer Mode shows it on localhost pages, with a Network button
  that opens DevTools on its Network panel; Site Controls turns it on or off per site. Thanks,
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2648).

## Fixed

- **Little Ardia's shortcut no longer takes ⌥⌘N away from other apps.** Since 0.2.47-nightly.7 Ardia claimed the key
  system-wide while it was in the background. It now stays inside Ardia unless you turn that on in Settings.
- **⌘W on the New Tab beside running Top Apps closes the window.** With "Keep Top Apps running" on, every ⌘W made
  another New Tab and the window never closed.
- **⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab step through tabs again.**
- **Google Meet shows your own preview again while you share your screen.**
- **Clicking Ardia in Stage Manager opens it at once** instead of bouncing it back into the strip.
- **The New Tab row no longer stays docked at the bottom of the sidebar after the display changes**, and its hover
  highlight lets go when the window moves.
- **Reopen Closed Tab puts tabs closed together back in their places.** Thanks,
  [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2504).

## Smaller

- The command bar has Show Tabs Across the Top and Show Tabs in Sidebar. Thanks, [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1864).
- The ⌃Tab switcher follows light mode. Thanks, [@Paikan](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2770).
- Text sits optically centred beside icons, and folders with no colour of their own take the profile's. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2877).
- `config.json` can bring back 0.2.47-nightly.6's press bounce and folder look. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2764).
- The Back button says "Close Tab and Go Back" when Back closes the tab, and the sidebar slides on a smoother curve.
- Picture in Picture shows resize arrows wherever a press resizes the window.
