## New

- **Plugins.** Drop a folder with a `plugin.json` into Ardia's plugins folder and it can add settings, command bar
  commands, a page that opens as a tab or beside one, and a sandboxed helper program. Every plugin arrives off: turning
  it on shows exactly what it will do, and approves that exact `plugin.json`. Settings-only plugins from
  github.com/ardia-browser/plugins keep working. On the Mac only for now.
- **Settings has eight panes instead of sixteen.** General, Profiles, Tabs, Appearance, Automations, Shortcuts,
  Plugins and Advanced, with one line per row and a look closer to System Settings. A row that only matters when another setting is
  on appears only then; search still finds it and says what to turn on. Some rarely used switches are gone, among them
  ⌘-scroll zoom, page scroll bars and the Translate button switch. Thanks,
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2362).
- **Imports bring more with them.** Dia's folders, nesting, favourites, spaces and every profile's tabs come over
  instead of one flat "Imported" list. Arc's Air Traffic Control rules, archived tabs and space icons come too, and its
  folders arrive closed. Firefox and Zen bookmarks and tabs keep their icons, and the last screen lists the Chrome Web
  Store extensions each profile had.
- **Old devices and deleted profiles can leave sync.** Settings › General lists each synced device and each profile
  that isn't on this Mac with Remove…, which takes it off every Mac and the iPhone. One that still syncs comes back.
  Thanks, [@yagoinved](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1578).
- **Sleep a tab or a whole group from its menu.** Sleep Tab and Sleep Tabs put hidden tabs to sleep; a tab with an
  edited form or playing media stays awake. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2220).
- **A pill points to the selected tab when it's scrolled out of the sidebar**, as in Dia. Click it and the list glides
  there. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2310).
- **Rows, tiles and buttons give a little when pressed**, and hover highlights fade out instead of switching off.
  Reduce Motion turns the press off.
- **The Playing now card can follow you across profiles.** Settings › Tabs › Keep Playing now across profiles shows it
  in every profile of the window, as Arc does across spaces. Off by default.
- **Pages can get a head start.** Settings › Profiles › Preload pages lets links open faster, at the cost of fetching
  pages you may never visit. Off by default, per profile.
- **Little Ardia's shortcut works from any app.** ⌥⌘N opens it with Ardia in the background, over a full-screen app
  too.
- **The sidebar slides out over a full-screen page.** With a video in full screen, the pointer at the window's edge
  brings the sidebar out; the page stays full screen underneath.
- **⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab step down and up the tabs**, as in Chrome. The recent-tabs switcher has no key until you give it
  one in Settings › Shortcuts.
- **Help › Copy Last Freeze.** Ardia keeps a short record of the last 20 times it stopped responding, on your Mac, and
  this copies the latest for a bug report.
- **Double-click a split divider to even the panes.** Thanks,
  [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1705).
- **`ardia browse` wastes fewer steps.** `open <url> --read` opens a page and prints its text in one go, a click
  closes a site's pop-up through its own close button, and a tab whose page is stuck fails in about 3 seconds instead
  of 30.
- **Linux installs like a Linux app.** The .deb registers Ardia as a browser, every release carries an Arch package to
  build with `makepkg`, and the window has its buttons at the top right. On Windows, a window reopens where you left
  it, maximized if it was, and autoplay can be blocked as on the Mac. Thanks,
  [@HarshitRuwali](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2237) and
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2350).

## Faster

- **A tab you press in the sidebar shows its page at once, even when Ardia is busy.** The page used to wait for
  Ardia's interface to catch up. From press to page it went from 13 ms to 5 ms when idle, from 496.5 ms to 1 ms with
  Ardia busy for half a second, and from 1,997.5 ms to 1 ms with it busy for two seconds. This covers sidebar rows and
  pinned tiles, not keyboard shortcuts, and for that moment typing and clicks aimed at the page are ignored.
- **A long tab list scrolls without dropped frames with the pointer on it.** Thanks,
  [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2007).
- **Ardia does less work while you browse.** A title or icon change no longer saves the whole session or wakes every
  row in the sidebar, a long history costs less on each visit and each sync, and a loading tab's pulsing stop button
  no longer keeps the app awake. Closing or opening a tab slides only the rows you can see. Thanks,
  [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2181).
- **Switching desktops shows the page at once.** A window covered by others, or left on another desktop, keeps its
  last frame instead of going blank for 2 to 3 seconds when you come back. It lets the frame go after 10 minutes, or
  sooner when memory is short.
- **Switching profiles with many tabs no longer leaves the old page beside the new sidebar.** Thanks,
  [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/679).

## Fixed

- **Settings no longer crash Ardia.** In 0.2.47-nightly.2 to nightly.5, picking a default search engine, changing a
  site permission or clearing browsing data in Chrome's settings pages could quit the browser.
- **The sidebar no longer freezes for 40 seconds after days of use.** Tabs wouldn't switch and shortcuts did nothing
  every few minutes, while pages stayed live. Ardia kept every old copy of its tab list in memory, and tidying them up
  took longer each day. Thanks, [@dotyigit](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2185).
- **Unlocking Settings › Passwords with thousands of logins no longer freezes Ardia.**
- **A rare video upscaling crash is fixed.**
- **Hover and the keyboard no longer get stuck until a restart.** After a few minutes ⌘A and the arrow keys could
  chime and pages ignore the pointer while clicks still worked.
- **The pointer comes back over Ardia's own interface** after a video or a game hid it.
- **Typing right after ⌘T is kept when Ardia is busy**, instead of beeping or landing in the old page.
- **Pasting works after another app hands the keyboard back**, as Raycast's clipboard and emoji pickers do.
- **The New Tab field no longer comes back over the page you switched to.** Thanks,
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2502).
- **Closing a tab no longer leaves a blank row in the sidebar**, and a click in the thin gap between two rows opens
  the nearer one.
- **Sidebar rows keep their hover and close buttons** after the command bar opens in a window with the address in the
  sidebar.
- **Spotify plays in WebKit tabs.** The player sat at 0:00 with no sound because the content blocker caught its
  songs.
- **⌘Tab returns to the browser window, not the floating video.** Thanks,
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2580) and
  [@fastbyt3](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1773).
- **Little Ardia stays out of the way.** A link from another app no longer flashes the main window first, closing it
  after you came back to Ardia keeps you in Ardia, and it fits the display it lands on. Thanks,
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2573) and
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2510).
- **Extensions behave in more places.** A second profile keeps its pinned icons after a relaunch, Settings opens the
  Web Store in the profile it shows, and an extension's side panel keeps running when you switch tabs. Thanks,
  [@Ontrei](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1725),
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2210) and
  [@LaganYT](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/549).
- **Closing a pinned tab that was playing takes its Playing now card with it.**
- **A full-screen window returns to full screen on the display it was on** after a relaunch.
- **Dragging a window is smooth on a 60 Hz display**, and dragging the top edge resizes the window instead of moving
  it. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2340).
- **A mouse wheel scrolls the auto-hidden sidebar again**, and the traffic lights no longer stay behind after it
  hides. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2330).
- **A ⌘-click that moves a little no longer opens nothing and then several copies of the tab.**
- **A tab opened from a link takes the keyboard** without a click on the page first.
- **WebKit tabs keep a text field's focus when you switch away and back**, and 英数 and かな switch the input source
  there.
- **Google's Endpoint Verification reports the device again**, so sites behind context-aware access open.
- **GitHub live folders say when they'll update again** when GitHub limits requests, instead of asking you to sign back
  in, and they ask GitHub for far less.

## Smaller

- ⌥⇧⌘V pastes and matches style, as on the rest of macOS. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2369).
- ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo typing in the command bar.
- ⌘1 to ⌘9 count a collapsed group as one row, and ⇧⌘C with several tabs selected copies every link.
- ⇧⌘C toggles Select an Element when Developer Tools has the focus.
- ⌘W closes About Ardia, not the tab behind it.
- Keyboard Shortcuts accepts Option-only shortcuts such as ⌥C.
- Copying the command bar's whole field copies the full address, `https://` included. Thanks, [@Bhanu1776](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2359).
- The command bar offers Google Maps for "maps", finds renamed tabs by name and keeps a typed link's #fragment.
- The centred command bar centres on the window and no longer jumps when the sidebar hides.
- Late suggestions wait while the pointer is over the list or Go, so a click lands where you aimed.
- With the address in the sidebar, the command bar opens on the side the sidebar is on.
- Reader, Translate and Zoom in the address bar hide until you hover it, like the other buttons.
- The tab hover card shows the site's domain; Settings › Tabs › Show full address in tab hover menu brings the address back. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2311).
- Settings › Tabs › Make folders in Top Apps turns off folders made by holding one tile over another. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2312).
- The tab switcher keeps its cards still under the pointer and scrolls only at the edges. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2556).
- A tab that hasn't painted yet keeps the profile colour behind it. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2446).
- A renamed pinned tab keeps its name after a link inside its site.
- Live folders show their service's icon again, and a group with no colour gets a neutral folder, not a purple one.
- A live folder's header hovers like a tab group's, and its refresh button fades. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2217).
- A pull request's status badge stays in its row. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2174).
- Hover shows on glass Top Apps tiles, and the unread count no longer covers the icon.
- Hovered tabs no longer touch the divider's Pinned, Tidy and Clear labels. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1600).
- Profile badge letters and emoji sit on the circle's centre. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2584).
- Peek and Little Ardia keep their corner rounding while you zoom them.
- A ⇧-clicked link inside a mail message's frame opens a Peek, not a window.
- A tab that a page opens onto a page uBlock Origin blocked opens behind it.
- Opening a downloaded zip shows it, and its unpacked folder, in Finder.
- A download shows its speed when the server sends no size, as in Chrome. Thanks, [@vlzuiev](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1731).
- Pulling a swipe back after the history list opens cancels it again.
- A wheel mouse's sideways scroll pages spaces the way it scrolls.
- Menus outside a tab no longer show "Open Link as".
- An untouched starter profile takes the name of the first Arc or Zen space you import.
- The onboarding pin preview shows three tiles a row, like the sidebar.

## Not yet

- **Netflix, Disney+, YouTube movies and Spotify no longer play in Chromium.** 0.2.47-nightly.4 and nightly.5 offered
  it under Settings › Engines; the setting is gone, because it made a Chromium tab pass as Safari, which the services'
  terms and Apple's FairPlay licensing don't allow. They open in WebKit, as before.
