date: 2026-11-25
headline: Fewer freezes, and you choose what comes after a close.
---

## New

- **Pick the tab that comes forward after you close one.** Settings › Appearance › Switching tabs offers the tab you
  used last, the tab above, or the tab below (to the right with tabs across the top), with a picture of each. Thanks,
  [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3871) and
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3909).
- **A new welcome.** Onboarding opens with a full-screen intro, then asks its questions in one scrolling column beside
  a live preview of your browser. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4157).
- **Tabs across the top keep every tab in the strip.** The strip scrolls with the trackpad or a wheel instead of
  moving tabs into the Open Tabs menu, and the tab you switch to scrolls into view.
- **Swipe the toolbar to change tabs.** A two-finger swipe on the toolbar, or on the address bar when it sits in the
  sidebar, goes to the tab above or below.
- **Open tabs come before search suggestions.** An open tab that matches what you type is listed above the search
  engine's suggestions, and in the ⌘T bar an address another tab already shows is offered as Switch to Tab first.
- **A new screen-share picker.** When a site asks to share your screen, one sheet with Tabs, Windows and Entire Screen
  shows a real preview of each. Thanks, [@itsOmSarraf](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2791).
- **Ardia has a new painted app icon.**
- **Clear Downloads from the Downloads button.** Right-click it for Show Downloads and Clear Downloads, which removes
  the finished downloads and leaves the ones still running.
- **Ardia tells you when a background tab keeps the CPU busy.** Put it to sleep or keep it running; a switch in
  Settings turns the warning off.

## Fixed

- **Five freezes that arrived with 0.2.48 are gone.** Ardia no longer stalls while it reads engine replies, opens
  files, checks WebKit cookies or plays the intro music.
- **Your tabs survive a damaged session file.** When the file that holds your windows, tabs and pins was empty, cut
  short or unreadable, Ardia saved a near-empty session over it soon after launch. It now keeps the damaged copy aside
  and restores the latest daily backup, and an older build you go back to never writes over a newer build's session.
- **One bad pin entry no longer crashes every launch.** Thanks,
  [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3548).
- **⌘Z after the command bar closed no longer crashes Ardia.**
- **Dragging the window works again on macOS 27.2 and later,** so tiling, snapping and moving it to another Space work.
- **Hover and the text cursor keep working after a click brings Ardia forward.** For some people, a click that brought
  Ardia to the front left hover highlights and the caret dead until they switched apps.
- **A WebKit tab in the background no longer blanks the tabs opened before it.** Thanks,
  [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3646).
- **The command bar holds still while you type.** The inline completion no longer blinks off and on with every key,
  and the search engine's late suggestions are added below instead of replacing the rows you were about to click.
- **A bookmark is found by its own name** in the command bar, even when the page is also in your history under another
  title. Thanks, [@ninadvyas](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2321).
- **Large folders and tab groups open and close without stalling.**
- **On x.com, search suggestions and @-mentions no longer get stuck loading.** Thanks,
  [@Deepusleepy](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3379).
- **You can turn off ⌘-scroll zoom again.** Thanks, [@prshv1](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4090).
- **A Picture in Picture window opened by switching apps stays open on the first press,** and pinch and ⌘-scroll reach
  it without a click first. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4117).

## Smaller

- ⌘-clicking a link no longer puts it in a tab group unless you turned that on yourself; the switch is still in
  Settings as "⌘-clicking links creates tab groups".
- Opening and closing a tab animates, quickly and plainly, in the sidebar and across the top.
- Hidden tabs go to sleep sooner when memory runs low, and tabs you use often, that played sound recently or that are
  downloading stay loaded.
- Updates download only what changed since a recent version, instead of the whole app.
- Turning on "Cycle through tabs in most recently used order" in Chrome's settings now changes ⌃Tab too, and the
  switcher's cards no longer flash white on dark pages.
- Double-clicking a pinned tile that's already on its page reloads it, as in Dia.
- Closing a pinned tab shows your next open pinned tab instead of jumping to the Tabs section. Thanks,
  [@seenimohamed](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2774).
- Remove from Group keeps the tabs in their sidebar order. Thanks,
  [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3443).
- An address that can't exist, like 192.168.0.256, searches instead of opening a page that can't load. Thanks,
  [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3450).
- Each route of a site like Roam (#/app/…) is its own page in the command bar, with its own title and icon.
- Settings opens with its content showing, without a blank window first, and takes your typing at once. Thanks,
  [@sai0x20charan](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3515).
- On Linux, Settings › Appearance › Window buttons can bring back the traffic lights or hide the buttons. Thanks,
  [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3409).
- On Linux, turning Sync on asks where its folder goes, and Settings can move it while Sync is on. Thanks,
  [@HarshitRuwali](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2932).
