## New

- **Netflix, Disney+ and YouTube movies play in Chromium.** With Settings › Engines › Play protected media in Chromium
  with FairPlay on, they stay in Chromium instead of moving to WebKit, with the site's own controls on top, Picture in
  Picture with skip buttons, and the display kept awake while they play. Thanks,
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1963).
- **Air Traffic Control can send a link into a tab group.** A rule that opens a profile as a tab can name one of that
  profile's groups; the link opens in the group, which expands. Thanks,
  [@LuisUrrutia](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/381).
- **Sync every open tab, not only pinned ones.** Settings › Sync › All Open Tabs keeps a profile's tabs and groups the
  same on your Macs.

## Fixed

- **Tabs on top stay where you scroll them.** Scrolling away from the start of the strip no longer jumps back to the
  selected tab, and with many tabs a scroll takes about a quarter less work. The edges fade smoothly across a tab
  instead of dimming whole tabs.
- **Sidebar rows don't light up as a scroll carries them under the pointer.** The hover waits for the scroll to stop.
- **The auto-hidden sidebar stays out in a full-screen window**, and a click on the sidebar or the title bar that brings
  Ardia forward focuses that window. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2141).
- **Video upscaling stays off in a tab you're sharing**, so Meet's presentation preview isn't black.
- **Memory Saver no longer puts a loading tab to sleep.**
- **A rare crash at launch is fixed.**

## Smaller

- Swiping with a single profile bounces back without redrawing the sidebar.
- A tab without a favicon shows its globe centred, as in Dia.
