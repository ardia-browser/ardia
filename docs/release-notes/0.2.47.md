## New

- **Ardia runs on Chromium 158.** The engine moves up from 157 to 158.0.8127.36, with Helium 0.22.1's privacy patches on top, and brings Chrome 158's security fixes. Your profiles, tabs, passwords, extensions and filters carry over as they are.
- **Plugins.** A plugin is a folder with a `plugin.json` that can add settings, command bar commands, a page that opens as a tab or beside one, and a helper program that runs in a sandbox. Every plugin arrives off; turning one on shows exactly what it will do and approves that exact file. Settings › Plugins lists them, with the official ones one click from installed; Mac only for now.
- **Apple Passwords, as an official plugin.** Turn it on under Settings › Plugins › Official and a key appears in sign-in fields; click it, or choose Edit › AutoFill › Passwords…, to open macOS's Passwords panel and fill with Touch ID. It works on Chrome-engine and WebKit pages, and can set up verification codes from a site's QR code. The panel lists all your logins, so Ardia shows a hint beside the key with what to type to find the site's. Thanks, [@kirolos-esmat](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1096).
- **The `ardia` command is now the Command Line plugin.** Install it under Settings › Plugins › Official to put `ardia` on your PATH; once installed it stays, with no switch. If you already used the command with control on, it carries over by itself. Its new `ardia call` and `ardia methods` reach the browser's own commands: folders, tiles, settings, extensions, sync, prompts and menus.
- **Settings has eight panes instead of sixteen.** General, Profiles, Tabs, Appearance, Automations, Shortcuts, Plugins and Advanced, one line per row, with a look closer to System Settings. Passwords, search engines, extensions and site settings live under each profile, and a row that only matters when another setting is on appears only then; search still finds it. Search also finds a shortcut by its keys: type ⌥⌘N or "cmd opt n". Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2362).
- **Links from other apps go where you send them**, like Arc's Air Traffic Control. Settings › Automations › Link routing sends them to the profile you pick, as a new tab, a new window, Little Ardia or into one of that profile's tab groups, by app and by site or pattern. Test a link shows where one would land before it arrives. Thanks, [@LuisUrrutia](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/381).
- **Imports bring more with them.** Dia's folders, nesting, favourites, spaces and every profile's tabs come over instead of one flat list, and Arc's link rules, archived tabs, space icons and default search engine come too, as does the default search engine from other Chromium browsers. Firefox and Zen bookmarks keep their icons, the last screen lists each profile's Web Store extensions, and on Windows and Linux onboarding offers Chromium, Vivaldi and Opera when it finds them. Thanks, [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1795).
- **Drag and drop works like Arc's.** Dragging in the sidebar shows a drop line and a see-through ghost; drop a tab on a row to split with it, or on another profile to move it there. Profile dots can be dragged to reorder profiles. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2734).
- **Closing a tab goes back to the tab you used before it**, as in Arc, instead of a neighbour. A sleeping tab isn't woken while an awake one is there to go back to. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/416).
- **Back on a tab a link opened closes it** and returns you to the tab that opened it; the Back button says "Close Tab and Go Back" when it will. Thanks, [@avoid-ashraful](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1401).
- **Memory stops growing with every open tab.** With Memory Saver on, once you have more loaded tabs than your Mac's memory suits, the ones hidden longest go to sleep after an hour out of sight; Chrome's own timer waited 2 to 6 hours, so with dozens of tabs almost none slept. Memory Saver's Maximum level also unloads profiles no window has shown for 15 minutes, and they load again when you open them. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1684) and [@Neved4](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1368).
- **Ardia's own interface can be bigger or smaller.** Settings › Appearance › "Interface size" scales the sidebar, toolbar, menus and Settings from 80% to 150% in 5% steps. Web pages keep their size.
- **Minimalism mode.** View › Minimalism Mode (⌃⌘M) leaves a column of tabs and the page: no toolbar, no address field or buttons in the sidebar, and pinned tiles as letters. ⌘T opens the command bar.
- **Reading mode.** ⌥⌘R, View › Show Reader or a button in the address field shows just the article, in five sizes, serif or sans. Closing it leaves the page where you were, with no reload.
- **Hide anything on a page.** View › Hide Element… (⇧⌘H) hides what you click on that site from then on, and View › Hidden Elements on This Site… (⇧⌘U) brings it back.
- **Tabs on top hold up with many tabs.** Tabs shrink to their icon before the strip scrolls, the selected tab keeps its title, and in a narrow window tabs that would shrink to a few letters move into the open tabs menu. The strip also gets the sidebar's tab menu and its menu of open and recently closed tabs. Thanks, [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1583).
- **⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab step down and up the tabs**, as in Chrome, instead of switching between your last two tabs. This is on purpose. The switcher that shows each recent tab as a picture of its page has no key until you give it one in Settings › Shortcuts. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1486).
- **Peek opens and closes with a Genie effect**, and Open as Tab carries the page over without a flash. Thanks, [@TechSupportz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2168).
- **Peek stays with its tab.** Switch away and back and the Peek is still there. Thanks, [@daberni](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1361).
- **Sync every open tab, and tidy the device list.** Settings › Profiles › "Sync All Open Tabs" keeps a profile's tabs and groups the same on your Macs, not only pinned ones. Settings › General › Sync lists each device and each profile that isn't on this Mac with Remove…, which takes it off every Mac and the iPhone. Thanks, [@yagoinved](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1578).
- **Block autoplay and notification requests everywhere.** Site settings, under Settings › Profiles, can stop videos in Chromium tabs except on sites you set to Allow, and turn every new notification request down without asking, in every profile. Site Controls gets an Autoplay row and Site Settings….
- **Add your own uBlock Origin filter lists.** Settings › General › Content blocking › Filter lists takes a list's address, keeps it updated with the others and shows how many filters it has.
- **Themes can change fonts, sizes, spacing, layout and icons**, not only colours, and a saved theme file applies at once.
- **Top Apps can keep running.** Settings › Tabs › "Keep Top Apps running when closed" makes ⌘W switch away from a Top App but keep its page loaded, so its notifications still arrive.
- **Pop-up windows can open as tabs.** Settings › Tabs › "Open pop-ups as tabs" opens a site's pop-up as a tab beside the page, from a Peek and from Little Ardia too.
- **New tabs can open an address you choose** instead of the New Tab page, under Settings › Tabs. Thanks, [@yagoinved](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1640).
- **Put links from other apps first.** Settings › Tabs puts links from Slack, Mail and the rest just below your pinned tabs. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/803).
- **Sleep a tab or a whole group from its menu.** A tab with an edited form or playing media stays awake. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2220).
- **A pill points to the selected tab when it's scrolled out of the sidebar**, as in Dia. Click it and the list glides there. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2310).
- **The Playing now card can follow you across profiles.** Settings › Tabs › "Keep Playing now across profiles" shows it in every profile of the window, as Arc does across spaces.
- **Pages can get a head start.** Settings › Profiles › "Preload pages" lets links open faster, at the cost of fetching pages you may never visit. Off by default.
- **The sidebar slides out over a full-screen page.** With a video in full screen, the pointer at the window's edge brings the sidebar out; the page stays full screen underneath.
- **Little Ardia from anywhere.** ⌥⌘N opens it from other apps, over a full-screen app too, once you turn on "Shortcut opens Little Ardia from other apps" in Settings › Tabs. Swipe back closes it when its page has nothing to go back to.
- **Split View marks the pane you're in** with a ring in the profile's colour. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1695).
- **Double-click a split divider to even the panes.** Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1705).
- **The command bar runs Arc's menu commands**, such as Inspect Elements, Task Manager and Manage Extensions. Thanks, [@Coeeter](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/452).
- **⌘T then Return goes back to your most recent tab** when new tabs open the command bar, and ↓ and ↑ move through the tabs without filling the field. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1355).
- **Groups and live folders show a folder** in their colour that opens with them, when they have no icon of their own. Thanks, [@mtbaqer](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/376).
- **Tidy works on the tabs below it and leaves your pins alone**, as in Arc. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/410).
- **Live folders can hide a pull request** from its row's hover button, and with Show Completed off a merged pull request's tab leaves the folder too. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/418).
- **The clean-up suggestion has Don't show again**, a shorter card and Undo; it used to come back every launch. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1336).
- **More ways to set the look.** Larger, higher-contrast sidebar text, the selected Top App as a ring, a fill or an outline, a switch for outlines around tiles and fields, and a Tab corners slider. Sleeping tabs can show their title struck through, and the sidebar's profile dots can be hidden. Thanks, [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1862) and [@felipeorlando](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/97).
- **The tab hover card waits for you.** It opens only when the pointer rests, goes the moment it leaves, shows the site's domain, and can be turned off in Settings › Tabs. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2311).
- **The auto-hidden sidebar is easier to reach and to keep.** It tells a reach for it from a pass along the edge, resizes by dragging, holds while you press on it, and the pointer stops at an edge your other display shares. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1816).
- **Picture in Picture skips 10 seconds** with a click on either side of the video, and Google Meet pops out when you leave its tab.
- **History keeps more.** Imported history stays past 90 days, the History page lists up to 100,000 pages with keyboard selection and ⇧-click ranges, and Recently Closed shows only the window's profile.
- **A loading pill at the top of the page** shows while a tab loads where the address field isn't visible. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1416).
- **Help › Copy Last Freeze.** Ardia keeps a short record of the last 20 times it stopped responding, on your Mac, and this copies the latest for a bug report.
- **`ardia browse` does more in fewer steps.** It can record a task once and rerun it as one request, `open <url> --read` opens a page and prints its text, `read --data` reads a page's embedded data, and a tab whose page is stuck fails in about 3 seconds instead of 30. A click behind a site's dialog fails instead of landing on the page. Thanks, [@BryanLe80](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1895).
- **Ardia for Linux.** The same sidebar, tabs, Peek, Split View and Little Ardia on x86-64, Wayland or X11, as a tarball, a .deb, an AppImage or an Arch package. Thanks, [@mckamyk](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/622), [@HarshitRuwali](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1869), [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1785) and [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2350).
- **Ardia for Windows, as an early build.** Installers for Intel and AMD PCs and for Arm PCs install it for your user with no admin prompt; it isn't signed yet, so Windows asks first (More info, then Run anyway).
- **A mouse button can be a shortcut.** Bind Middle Click, or buttons 4 and 5, to any command in Settings › Shortcuts, such as Close Tab. Mac only for now.


## Fixed

- **The sidebar no longer freezes for 40 seconds after days of use.** Every few minutes tabs wouldn't switch and shortcuts did nothing, while pages stayed live, because Ardia kept every old copy of its tab list and tidying them took longer each day. Thanks, [@dotyigit](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2185).
- **A tab you click in the sidebar shows its page at once, even when Ardia is busy.** The page used to wait for Ardia's interface to catch up, for up to two seconds; it now shows in about a millisecond. This covers sidebar rows and pinned tiles.
- **Unlocking Settings › Passwords with thousands of logins no longer freezes Ardia.**
- **Hover and the keyboard no longer get stuck until a restart.** After a few minutes ⌘A and the arrow keys could chime and pages ignored the pointer while clicks still worked.
- **A rare crash at launch and a rare video upscaling crash are fixed.**
- **Typing right after ⌘T is kept when Ardia is busy**, instead of beeping or landing in the old page.
- **Space and the arrow keys reach the page** after the command bar closes or you switch tabs with it open. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1894).
- **Pasting works after another app hands the keyboard back**, as Raycast's clipboard and emoji pickers do.
- **The pointer comes back over Ardia's own interface** after a video or a game hid it.
- **Switching desktops shows the page at once.** A window covered by others, or left on another desktop, went blank for 2 to 3 seconds when you came back; it now keeps its last frame for up to 10 minutes, less when memory is short.
- **Switching profiles with many tabs no longer leaves the old page beside the new sidebar.** Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/679).
- **⌘Tab returns to the browser window, not the floating video.** Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2580) and [@fastbyt3](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1773).
- **Little Ardia takes you back to the app you came from.** When its page opens an app it closes, and closing it brings back the app that sent the link. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1903).
- **Little Ardia stays out of the way.** A link from another app no longer brings the main window forward or flashes it first, and a new Little Ardia opens centred on the display it lands on. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2573) and [@shinokamix](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1837).
- **Closing Little Ardia after you came back to Ardia keeps you in Ardia**, and Esc closes a blank one too. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2510).
- **Extensions behave in more places.** A second profile keeps its pinned icons after a relaunch and an extension's side panel keeps running when you switch tabs. Thanks, [@Ontrei](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1725), [@LaganYT](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/549) and [@seenimohamed](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/549).
- **Extensions install into the profile you mean.** Settings opens the Web Store in the profile it shows, and Add installs in the profile a window shows, not the window's first. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2210).
- **Spotify plays in WebKit tabs.** The player sat at 0:00 with no sound because the content blocker caught its songs.
- **Screen sharing works with Google Meet.** Meet shows your own preview while you present, video upscaling stays off in the tab you share so its preview isn't black, and pages stay put as the sharing bar slides in and out.
- **Pages no longer stay blank** after a window of the same size opens over theirs.
- **Clicking the sidebar over a WebKit page no longer clicks the page**, and switching to a WebKit tab shows it at once. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1257).
- **WebKit tabs keep a text field's focus when you switch away and back**, and 英数 and かな switch the input source there.
- **Reopen Closed Tab puts tabs closed together back in their places.** Thanks, [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2504).
- **⌘T on an empty New Tab page opens another tab again**, as it did before 0.2.46, so you can open more than one at a time.
- **⌘S hides the tabs in its own window only**, as in Dia. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1619).
- **The media card keeps the site's time.** It follows looping videos, a preloaded next track and players that hand off to another element. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1618).
- **Pop-ups open over a full-screen window**, not on another desktop. Thanks, [@McSon2](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1741).
- **Settings, Import and Little Ardia float in tiling window managers** such as AeroSpace, instead of tiling. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1905) and [@c0nfsd](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1880).
- **The downloads panel behaves.** A click outside reaches the page, a quick second click opens it again, and quitting keeps a running download's progress. Thanks, [@mjkang-estrella](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1875) and [@FedyaLight](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1396).
- **Clear Browsing Data stays open with an error and Retry when clearing fails.** Thanks, [@mjkang-estrella](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1937).
- **Small windows work.** A window shrinks to 500 points wide, and the sidebar narrows so the toolbar and page keep their room.
- **Resizing and dragging windows is smooth.** ⌥ and ⌥⇧ resize from the centre without jumping, a drag keeps up on a 60 Hz display, and the top edge resizes instead of moving the window. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2340).
- **The auto-hidden sidebar stays out in a full-screen window**, and a click on it or the title bar brings that window forward. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2141).
- **The window buttons no longer sit over the page while the auto-hidden sidebar is away.** Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2352).
- **A full-screen window returns to full screen on its own display** after a relaunch.
- **A ⌘-click that moves a little no longer opens nothing and then several copies of the tab.**
- **A tab opened from a link takes the keyboard** without a click on the page first.
- **Closing a tab no longer leaves a blank row in the sidebar**, and a click in the thin gap between two rows opens the nearer one.
- **Sidebar rows don't light up as a scroll carries them under the pointer.**
- **Memory Saver no longer puts a loading tab to sleep.**
- **Sync no longer stops on a profile that holds back many items at once.**
- **Google's Endpoint Verification reports the device again**, so sites behind context-aware access open.
- **GitHub live folders say when they'll update again** when GitHub limits requests, instead of asking you to sign back in, and they ask GitHub for far less.
- **A `javascript:` bookmark runs in the page** instead of searching for its code.
- **Undo after cleaning up more than 100 tabs brings every one back.** Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1529).
- **⌘Y puts the cursor in History's search field.** Thanks, [@Josephh310](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1510).
- **In a narrow window, tabs across the top keep their titles when there's room**, and a tab group keeps a tab in the strip instead of moving all of them into the open tabs menu.
- **Settings you turned off in 0.2.46 stay off after the update:** ⌘-scroll zoom, sidebar media controls, text adapting to a custom theme's colour, and the bookmarks bar's Import button.
- **Closing a window no longer loses the pinned tabs and folders of a profile that only that window was showing.** Before a save that would remove pinned tabs, Ardia now writes a backup of them, and keeps your previous session if that backup can't be written, trying again. If it still can't when you quit, the last saved session comes back next time, without the changes you made since. Pins already lost this way can't be brought back by Ardia; a Time Machine copy of `session.json`, or another Mac whose sync was off, still has them.
- **Opening Settings at a place no longer lands on an old search.** Manage Extension, a site's or a profile's settings, Plugins and Shortcuts open where you asked, even with a search left in the field.

## Not yet

- **Apple Passwords can't show only the site's logins.** macOS's panel opens on every saved login, A to Z, and doesn't let a browser filter it; Ardia's hint beside the key says what to type in Passwords to find the site.
- **Passkeys saved in Passwords aren't offered yet.** On sites that open in Chromium, a security key or a passkey on your phone through the QR code still works.
- **Plugins are on the Mac only for now**, and so is Interface size. Passwords don't sync on Windows yet.
