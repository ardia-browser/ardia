date: 2026-11-26
headline: Panes stack, tabs sort, and Ardia freezes less often.

## New

- **Split View panes can stack.** Drop a tab on the top or bottom edge of a pane to put it above or below, and the split's button shows which way it's split. Drag a pane by its Split View button to swap it with another or dock it on a new side, or use the split menu's Add Right Split and Add Bottom Split. Open Split Pane Below is ⌃⇧- and Move Split Pane is ⌃⌥ and an arrow (Ctrl+Shift+\ and Alt+Shift+arrows on Linux and Windows). Thanks, [@prshv1](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4015).
- **Choose what ⌃Tab does.** Settings › Appearance › Switching tabs has "⌃Tab goes to" (Recent tabs or In order) and "Tab switcher shows" (Page previews, Compact list or None). Recent tabs with page previews is the default; if you chose to step through tabs, you keep that. A quick tap switches at once, and holding ⌃ shows the switcher. Closed pinned tabs and sleeping tabs are skipped, so ⌃Tab no longer wakes them.
- **Pick the tab that comes forward after you close one.** The same pane offers the tab you used last, the tab above, or the tab below (to the right with tabs across the top), with a picture of each. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3871) and [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3909).
- **Close Duplicate Tabs.** The sidebar's menu, the File menu and the command bar ("dedupe" finds it) close the extra copies of any page among your unpinned tabs. The selected copy stays, otherwise the one used last, and Undo brings the rest back. Thanks, [@mvanhorn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4408).
- **Sort Tabs by Site or by Title.** From the sidebar's menu, a group's menu or the command bar. Groups keep their places and pinned tabs don't move. Thanks, [@mvanhorn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4409).
- **Translate from the page's right-click menu, and sooner.** Right-click a page for Translate to your language, or Show Original. Short lines go to the translation model in batches, so a long page fills in about twice as fast, and one passage the model can't handle no longer undoes the whole translation. Mac only.
- **A new welcome.** Onboarding opens with a full-screen intro, then asks its questions in one scrolling column beside a live preview of your browser. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4157).
- **Tabs across the top keep every tab in the strip.** The strip scrolls with the trackpad or a wheel instead of moving tabs into the Open Tabs menu, and the tab you switch to scrolls into view.
- **Swipe the toolbar to change tabs.** A two-finger swipe on the toolbar, or on the address bar when it sits in the sidebar, goes to the tab above or below.
- **Open tabs come before search suggestions.** An open tab that matches what you type is listed above the search engine's suggestions, and in the ⌘T bar an address another tab already shows is offered as Switch to Tab first.
- **A new screen-share picker.** When a site asks to share your screen, one sheet with Tabs, Windows and Entire Screen shows a real preview of each. Thanks, [@itsOmSarraf](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2791).
- **Ardia has a new painted app icon**, with a flat version of each of the seven choices in Settings › App Icon. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3654).
- **Give an extension only the sites you choose.** An extension set to "On specific sites" lists its sites in Settings › Profiles › Extensions, with Add Site… and Remove. After you add the 1Password extension, Ardia shows how to connect it to the 1Password app.
- **Ardia tells you when a background tab keeps the CPU busy.** Put it to sleep or keep it running; a switch in Settings turns the warning off.
- **Clear Downloads from the Downloads button.** Right-click it for Show Downloads and Clear Downloads, which removes the finished downloads and leaves the ones still running.
- **Minimalism mode can show site logos.** Settings › Appearance › Minimalism tiles switches Top Apps and pinned tabs between Letters and Site Logos, and a tile's menu › Customize › Change Letters… gives it one or two letters of your own.
- **Ardia asks before another program takes control.** On the Mac, a program that isn't Ardia's own `ardia` command waits for Allow Once, Allow for 30 Minutes, Always Allow or Don't Allow. Settings › Advanced lists the programs you allowed, and removing one cuts it off.
- **On Linux, an .rpm and a Flatpak** join the tarball, .deb, AppImage and Arch package. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4239) and [@HarshitRuwali](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3634).
- **On Windows, Ardia is rebuilt on Chromium's own window**, as on Linux, and updates itself. Your tabs, profiles and settings move over the first time it starts. The maximize button works, Ardia groups with its Start menu shortcut on the taskbar, and background tabs run in Windows' efficiency mode, with calls and Picture in Picture kept at full speed.

## Fixed

- **Ardia freezes far less.** The stalls that arrived with 0.2.48 are gone: Ardia no longer hangs while it reads engine replies, opens files, checks WebKit cookies, plays the intro music or adds to your history. Typing fast in the command bar no longer freezes it, closing a window no longer waits on the graphics process, and large folders and tab groups open and close without stalling.
- **Four crashes are fixed.** Closing a tab while a site's sign-in dialog was open, pressing ⌘Z after the command bar closed, an extension's page restarting, and one bad pin entry that crashed every launch. Thanks, [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3548).
- **Your tabs survive a damaged session file.** When the file that holds your windows, tabs and pins was empty, cut short or unreadable, Ardia saved a near-empty session over it soon after launch. It now keeps the damaged copy aside and restores the latest daily backup, and an older version you go back to never writes over a newer one's session.
- **Sync no longer brings back what you deleted after a crash.** If Ardia quit right after Sync applied a change, before Chrome had saved it, a bookmark deleted on another Mac could return, and bookmarks added elsewhere could be deleted everywhere. Sync now notes what it's about to apply and finishes it on the next launch. Very large histories and folder trees also sync without stalling. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2803).
- **Importing again adds only what's new.** Each repeat of a Dia, Arc or other browser import wrapped the same bookmarks in another "Imported from" folder. Now pages you already bookmarked are left out and new ones go into the folder the first import made.
- **Hover, the caret and the keyboard keep working after you come back to Ardia.** For some people, a click that brought Ardia forward left hover highlights and the caret dead, and ⌘T opened the command bar with nowhere to type; a key press now fixes it too. A faded-out piece of Ardia's interface can no longer swallow hover and clicks on the page under it.
- **Dragging the window works again on macOS 27.2 and later,** so tiling, snapping and moving it to another Space work.
- **⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo typing** in the command bar and the Settings search field again.
- **⌘V pastes rich text and files into the address bar.** A clipboard holding only formatted text, a web page's HTML or a file used to beep; it now pastes the text or the file's address.
- **Peek shows its page at full size, and asks before you lose a form.** A Peek drew its page at about 90%; it now draws it 1:1. Closing a Peek whose page has unsaved changes asks first, as closing a tab does, and ⌘W closes a Peek even while its Find bar is open.
- **A WebKit page in full screen fills the screen.** It used to stay at its windowed size in a corner. Thanks, [@mxdhavgautam](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4259).
- **Streaming sites opened from a link play.** A tab or Peek another page opened onto a streaming site now moves to WebKit, as typed addresses and bookmarks already did.
- **Google Meet pops out when you leave its window or Space**, not only when you leave its tab.
- **Picture in Picture keeps its shape and size.** A video's floating window reopens at the size you left it, in the video's own shape, even after it was last on another display; one opened by switching apps stays open on the first press, and pinch and ⌘-scroll reach it without a click first. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4227).
- **A WebKit tab in the background no longer blanks the tabs opened before it.** Thanks, [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3646).
- **The command bar holds still while you type.** The inline completion no longer blinks off and on with every key, and the search engine's late suggestions are added below instead of replacing the rows you were about to click.
- **A bookmark is found by its own name** in the command bar, even when the page is also in your history under another title. Thanks, [@ninadvyas](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/2321).
- **On x.com, search suggestions and @-mentions no longer get stuck loading.** Thanks, [@Deepusleepy](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3379).
- **You can turn off ⌘-scroll zoom again.** Thanks, [@prshv1](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/4090).
- **Closing a window that showed two profiles keeps each one's Top Apps folder.** Thanks, [@HiberNuts](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3286).
- **A pins backup Ardia can't read is kept, not replaced.** Thanks, [@Arav-Arun](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3280).
- **Clearing the last hour in WebKit pages keeps older choices.** It used to forget every site permission, app-opening allowance and HSTS entry, however old.
- **Closing the tab in front no longer wakes the tab you just put to sleep.** Thanks, [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3454).
- **Sync All Open Tabs forgets the pages of tabs you closed.** Thanks, [@Pruthvirajcodes](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/3557).
- **In light mode, the caret and the inline completion are easy to see** in every field.
- **Ardia runs on Chromium 158.0.8127.88**, with Helium 0.22.4's privacy patches and its canvas noise fixes underneath, and Chrome's latest security fixes. Sites can open files and folders they saved access to before Chromium 158 again.

## Smaller

- A second ⌘L closes the command bar and hands the keyboard back to the page, as in Arc.
- With only the selected tab left, Clear closes it too, as in Arc; tabs playing sound still stay.
- Esc closes a new, empty split pane, and typing in one lists the command bar's suggestions.
- Opening and closing a tab animates, quickly and plainly, in the sidebar and across the top.
- Hidden tabs go to sleep sooner when memory runs low, and tabs you use often, that played sound recently or that are downloading stay loaded.
- Updates download only what changed since a recent version, instead of the whole app.
- Double-clicking a pinned tile that's already on its page reloads it, as in Dia.
