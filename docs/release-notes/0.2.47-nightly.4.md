date: 2026-11-16
headline: The Mac catches up, at whatever size you like.
---

0.2.47-nightly.3 reached Linux and Windows only, so this is the Mac's first nightly since 0.2.47-nightly.2, and it
carries everything both builds changed.

## New

- **Ardia's own interface can be bigger or smaller.** Settings › Appearance › Interface Size scales the sidebar,
  toolbar, menus and Settings from 80% to 150% in 5% steps, as soon as you pick one. Web pages keep their size. On the
  Mac only for now.
- **Tabs on top stay usable with many tabs.** Tabs shrink to their icon before the strip scrolls, the selected tab keeps
  room for its title, and a tab that shares its icon with another keeps a title so you can tell them apart. Tabs fade
  at an edge that hides more, and dragging a tab to that edge scrolls the strip. The strip also gets the sidebar's tab
  menu and its menu of open and recently closed tabs. Thanks, [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1583).
- **Minimalism mode.** Settings › Appearance › Minimalism, View › Minimalism Mode or ⌃⌘M leaves a column of tabs and the
  page: no toolbar, no address field or buttons in the sidebar, and pinned tiles as letters. ⌘T opens the command bar.
- **Hide anything on a page.** View › Hide Element… (⇧⌘H) or the page menu lets you click an element to hide it on that
  site from then on. View › Hidden Elements on This Site… (⇧⌘U) and Settings › Privacy › Hidden elements bring them
  back.
- **Reading mode.** ⌥⌘R, View › Show Reader, the page menu or a button in the address field shows just the article, in
  five sizes, serif or sans. Closing it leaves the page where you were, with no reload.
- **Memory stops growing with every open tab.** With Memory Saver on, once you have more loaded tabs than your Mac's
  memory suits (about one per GB on Balanced, never fewer than 10), the tabs hidden longest go to sleep after an hour
  out of sight. Chrome's own timer waited 2 to 6 hours, so with dozens of tabs almost none ever slept.
- **Profiles you aren't using can unload.** Settings › Advanced › Performance › Unload inactive profiles frees a
  profile no window has shown for 5 minutes to 2 hours: its pages, extensions and ad blocker stop, its tabs stay in the
  sidebar, and everything loads again when you open it. Off by default; it needs Memory Saver.
- **Themes can change fonts, sizes, spacing, layout and icons**, not only colours, and a saved theme file applies at
  once. `ardia theme set`, `get` and `unset` restyle Ardia from the terminal one key at a time.
- **Add your own uBlock Origin filter lists.** Settings › Privacy › Advanced takes a list's address, downloads it, keeps
  it updated with the others and shows how many filters it has.
- **Block autoplay and notification requests everywhere.** Settings › Privacy & Security › Block autoplay on every site
  stops videos except on sites you set to Allow, and Block requests to send notifications turns every new request down
  without asking. Site Controls gets an Autoplay row and Site Settings…, which opens every setting the site has.
- **Spotify can play in a Chromium tab.** Settings › Engines › Play protected media in Chromium with FairPlay plays
  Spotify's protected tracks in Chromium instead of sending Spotify to WebKit. Off by default. Thanks,
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1936).
- **Pop-up windows can open as tabs.** Settings › Tabs › Links opens a site's pop-up as a tab beside the page, which
  stays open, from a Peek and from Little Ardia too.
- **New tabs can open an address you choose** instead of the New Tab page.
- **More ways to set the look.** Settings › Appearance adds larger, higher-contrast sidebar text, the selected Top App
  as a ring, a fill or an outline, a switch for the outlines around tiles and fields, and a Tab corners slider under
  Page Frame. Sleeping tabs can show their title struck through, and the sidebar's profile dots can be hidden. Thanks,
  [@Neel2107](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1862) and
  [@felipeorlando](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/97).
- **The address and command bars do what you set.** Site Controls, Split View and Bookmark can stay shown without
  hovering, the Translate button can go, the command bar can stop suggesting pages from history, and "In the centre,
  also on click" moves a click on the address to the centre too.
- **The tab hover card waits for you.** It opens only when the pointer rests, goes the moment it leaves, and can be
  turned off; everything it offered is in the tab's right-click menu. Turned off, resting on a tab shows its full title
  and site as a tooltip.
- **The auto-hidden sidebar is easier to reach and to keep.** It slides out only as far as its width, tells a reach for
  it from a pass along the edge, resizes by dragging, and holds while you press anywhere on it. A switch turns off
  reveal on hover, and the pointer stops at an edge your other display shares. Thanks,
  [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1816).
- **Peek stays with its tab.** Switch away and back and the Peek is still there, and its buttons can sit in the title
  bar, on the left or on the right. Thanks, [@daberni](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1361) and
  [@remixtedi](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1576).
- **Picture in Picture skips 10 seconds** with a click on the left or right side of the video, and Google Meet pops out
  when you leave its tab.
- **A loading pill at the top of the page** shows while a tab loads. Thanks,
  [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1416).
- **The command bar runs Arc's menu commands**, such as Inspect Elements, Task Manager and Manage Extensions. Thanks,
  [@Coeeter](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/452).
- **Drag pinned extensions to reorder the toolbar**, and Move to Front brings one that doesn't fit into the sidebar's
  address field.
- **Groups and live folders show a folder.** One without its own icon gets a folder in its colour that opens with it,
  and a live folder's Color submenu tints it like a tab group. Thanks,
  [@mtbaqer](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/376).
- **History keeps more.** Imported history stays past 90 days and the History page lists up to 100,000 pages, with rows
  you can select from the keyboard and Shift-click for a range. Recently Closed shows only the window's profile and ends
  with Clear Recently Closed.
- **Onboarding walks through every step again**, with the newer card and preview, and offers Chromium, Vivaldi and Opera
  when it finds them.
- **The `ardia` command sets itself up.** Ardia installs it and turns on control the first time. `ardia browse` can
  record a task once and rerun it as one request, read a page's embedded data with `read --data`, save rows to a
  `.csv`, and skip a screenshot that hasn't changed. An agent starts in the background and never takes your tab, and
  `ardia skills get` prints the skill that ships with it.
- **Linux gets a .deb and an AppImage.** Check for Updates works there, sites can ask for your location, and the intro
  plays its music. On Windows, dragging inside a page no longer moves the page off its window.

## Fixed

- **Space and the arrow keys reach the page again** after the command bar closes or you switch tabs with it open.
  Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1894).
- **Clear Browsing Data stays open with an error and Retry when clearing fails.** Thanks,
  [@mjkang-estrella](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1937).
- **Little Ardia takes you back to the app you came from.** When its page opens an app it closes, and closing it brings
  back the app that sent the link, for each window. A link from another app no longer brings the main window forward,
  and a new Little Ardia opens centred. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1903),
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1906) and
  [@shinokamix](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1837).
- **Pages no longer stay blank** after a window of the same size opens over theirs.
- **Pages stay put when the screen-sharing bar slides in and out**, and the sidebar marks the tab being shared.
- **The media card keeps the site's time.** It follows looping videos, a preloaded next track and players that hand off
  to another element, and grows and shrinks smoothly on hover. Thanks,
  [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1618).
- **Blocked autoplay stays blocked** in background tabs and in tabs opened from the same site.
- **Pop-ups open over a full-screen window**, not on another desktop. Thanks,
  [@McSon2](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1741).
- **Clicking the sidebar over a WebKit page no longer clicks the page**, and switching to a WebKit tab shows it at
  once. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1257).
- **A `javascript:` bookmark runs in the page** instead of searching Google for its code.
- **Small windows work.** A window shrinks to 500 points wide, half of a smaller display, and the sidebar narrows so
  the toolbar and page keep their room.
- **Settings, Import and Little Ardia float in tiling window managers** such as AeroSpace, instead of tiling. Thanks,
  [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1905).
- **The downloads panel behaves.** It opens for a new download but lets a click outside reach the page, a quick second
  click opens it again, and quitting keeps a running download's progress. Thanks,
  [@mjkang-estrella](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1875).
- **Resizing is smooth.** ⌥ and ⌥⇧ resize a window from its centre without jumping, and the sidebar's edge stays under
  the pointer for the whole drag.
- **The auto-hidden toolbar** shows the page's progress bar while it loads, and a feed that snaps as you scroll no
  longer collapses it.
